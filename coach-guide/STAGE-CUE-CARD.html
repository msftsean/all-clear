All Clear Facilitator Cue Card

90-minute incident-triage exercise · fictional scenarios only

0:00–0:10 · OPEN

Set the frame and safety boundary

Say: “We are practicing safe coordination with fictional inputs. All Clear supports human judgment; real safety concerns use the organization’s emergency procedure.”

0:10–0:25 · SURGE TRIAGE

Separate signals, reports, and incidents

Say: “A signal is an observation, a report is an account, and an incident is our managed response record. What evidence is known, duplicated, or still uncertain?”

0:25–0:40 · SEVERITY & SLA

Choose a response posture

Say: “Severity follows credible scope and risk. The SLA tells us when acknowledgement and the next update are due.”

0:40–1:00 · CLEARBOARD

Make the handoff visible

Say: “Record the evidence, magnitude, severity, owner, status, and next update. Could another responder act from this board alone?”

1:00–1:15 · VOICE & PHONE

Use accessible intake

Say: “Confirm essential details, offer text access, and create the same traceable record regardless of how the report arrives.”

1:15–1:30 · SAFETY & CLOSE

Practice the override

Say: “When safety is at stake, pause and escalate to the designated human responder. Good triage leaves a clear, safe next action.”
