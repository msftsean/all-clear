# All Clear Facilitator Troubleshooting

Use fictional exercise data only. If a real safety concern appears, stop troubleshooting and follow the designated emergency procedure.

## Triage and ClearBoard

### Duplicate incident records

- **Symptom:** Two or more records describe the same event.
- **Cause:** Separate reports were triaged before corroboration was checked.
- **Quick fix:** Select the incident with the clearest timeline, link the duplicates, preserve their sources, and post one status update explaining the merge.

### Severity dispute

- **Symptom:** Participants disagree on the severity level.
- **Cause:** The group is using labels without referring to evidence or the local policy.
- **Quick fix:** Read the severity definitions, list confirmed scope and risk, choose the current level, then set a time to reassess when the missing evidence arrives.

### No visible owner or next update

- **Symptom:** ClearBoard shows an active incident but no accountable action.
- **Cause:** The record was opened without a handoff.
- **Quick fix:** Assign the designated role, add the next update time, and state the immediate action in a concise board note.

### Magnitude is speculative

- **Symptom:** Impact language is broad but cannot be traced to a source.
- **Cause:** Assumptions have been recorded as facts.
- **Quick fix:** Replace the claim with source, timestamp, confidence, affected scope, and unknowns. Escalate the severity only when credible evidence supports it.

## Voice and phone intake

### Microphone permission is denied

- **Symptom:** The voice control cannot hear the caller.
- **Cause:** Browser permission, headset selection, or an unsupported context.
- **Quick fix:** Check the browser permission indicator, select the correct input, and offer the text intake path immediately. Do not make voice a requirement.

### Phone or voice service is unavailable

- **Symptom:** The demonstration cannot connect or playback is unreliable.
- **Cause:** Connectivity, device, or service interruption.
- **Quick fix:** Use the approved fictional transcript, demonstrate the confirmation and ClearBoard handoff, and keep the accessibility lesson intact. Never place a live emergency call for a demonstration.

### Spoken details are incomplete

- **Symptom:** A caller gives a partial location, unclear contact method, or rapidly changing facts.
- **Cause:** Audio intake increases ambiguity.
- **Quick fix:** Repeat back essential details, label unverified information, ask only the next necessary question, and record the source and time.

## Safety and facilitation

### A participant introduces a real safety concern

- **Symptom:** Content indicates imminent risk or a real person may need urgent help.
- **Cause:** The exercise has crossed into a real-world situation.
- **Quick fix:** Pause the session, contact the designated human responder or emergency service according to local procedure, and protect privacy. Do not continue role-play, diagnose, or promise an outcome.

### The group is stuck debating labels

- **Symptom:** Time passes with no owner or response action.
- **Cause:** Classification has replaced triage.
- **Quick fix:** Ask, “What is the safest reversible next action?” Assign it, set the next update, and revisit the label with new evidence.

### ClearBoard is not available

- **Symptom:** The shared board will not load.
- **Cause:** Access or connectivity failure.
- **Quick fix:** Use a projected static template with the same fields: evidence, magnitude, severity, SLA, owner, status, and next update. Enter the exercise record later if permitted.
