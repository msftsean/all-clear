What you're going to build

All Clear is an incident-triage pipeline: a signal comes in, QueryAgent classifies it, RouterExecutor makes a deterministic routing decision, and ActionAgent acts through bounded tools with grounded output.

Six required blocks, one verifiable path. You will run the system in mock mode, inspect the authority boundaries, write one focused spec, ground retrieval, exercise a surge, and finish with a small GitHub workflow. No deployed URL is required.

the required proof

Every required block ends with an observable checkpoint: a command, a reviewed artifact, or a test result.

how you work

Use a Codespace or local checkout. The exact participant instructions live under labs/; this workbook is the timed map and checklist.

What completion means

Completion means the required checkpoints are evidenced. Cloud deployment, APIM, MCP, voice, and phone are separate extensions and may be skipped.

★ Make It Yours optional reflection

Use the incident-triage vocabulary to sketch how a surge appears in your own work. This is a discussion prompt, not a required submission or data-collection step.

  1. Answer the three reflection prompts for your own work; keep the discussion hypothetical and local:
prompt 1 · the surge

What's your surge? The flood of inbound your team faces — calls, tickets, alerts, reports.

prompt 2 · the signals

What signals flood you? The raw messages that pile up faster than people can triage them.

prompt 3 · the incident

What's the real incident underneath? The handful of things that actually matter once the noise collapses.

Keep data local

Discuss or write your reflection locally. Do not enter real personal, operational, or agency-sensitive data into an unapproved service.
