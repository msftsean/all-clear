08 / Q&A — answers to this room’s concerns

QuestionVerbatim answer
“How do I stop it going off task?”“Use a small task, an explicit output contract, restricted tools and independent checks. Instructions help direct it; application permissions and tests are what let us enforce and verify boundaries. You should not have to rely on politeness or confidence.”
“Is every decision deterministic?”“No. Classification and semantic embeddings involve model behavior. Given their outputs and incident state, the router applies fixed dedup, severity and escalation rules. We still need to evaluate the inputs and the complete workflow.”
“Is this replacing our staff?”“The objective here is to remove duplicate handling and improve the human work queue. We would measure staff experience and review burden, not assume a staffing reduction is the outcome.”
“Can we use legal, student or patient data?”“This demonstration is not approval to do that. We would first review the data flows, access, retention, privacy requirements and the authorized use case with your institution. No sensitive case, student or patient records are needed for this exercise.”
“Are you CJIS / HIPAA / FERPA compliant?”“This demo establishes no compliance certification. The code includes safeguards, but compliance depends on the complete deployment, agreements, operations and your institution’s review.”
“Does the red card prove safety?”“No. A safety service may block an input, or it may proceed through the normal application rules. One probe cannot certify a system. We inspect the actual outcome and keep broader evaluation separate.”
“Is Foundry IQ doing this?”“I have not demonstrated Foundry IQ, Fabric or Work IQ here. This segment shows an incident pipeline, its AI platform connection, and engineering verification. Those integrations would need their own evidence.”
“Where does Copilot run the incident?”“It does not. Copilot is assisting the developer. The backend runs the All Clear pipeline. That separation is intentional.”
“Did Copilot write all this?”“Copilot is part of the development workflow. I am not claiming every line or today’s deployment was produced autonomously. The specification, implementation and verification still need accountable review.”
“Why does reset not remove old incidents?”“Reset clears the room tally, not operational history. A new room report can still attach to a retained incident. That is why I distinguish this room’s counts from lifetime incident magnitude.”
“What would ROI look like?”“We would baseline duplicate handling, review time, routing accuracy and staff experience for one workflow, then compare the pilot. The synthetic replay’s collapse ratio is not a financial result.”
